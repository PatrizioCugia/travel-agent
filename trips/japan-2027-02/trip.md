# Japan February–March 2027

Two travellers — Patrizio and his sister. 18–19 nights, late February into
early March. Dates not locked; the one fixed point is Nara (see below).

| Additional countryside | 2–3 (undecided) |

**The fixed point.** The Nara night must fall inside **1–14 March 2027**:
Omizutori (Shuni-e) at Todai-ji's Nigatsudō. Ten monks carry six-metre burning
torches along the veranda and shower embers on the crowd below, 19:00 nightly;
12 March is the largest night (eleven torches, 19:30) and correspondingly the
most crowded, 14 March the earliest (18:30). A ceremony unbroken since 752.
This is the trip's singular experience, and it is the reason Nara lodging is
the highest-urgency thing to watch — the town has little of it and this is its
busiest fortnight of the year.

**Countryside slots, undecided.** Wanted for scenery, temples and onsen. Two
seasonal facts that bear on the choice: matsuba crab season in Kinosaki Onsen
runs to roughly 20 March, and this is plum season, not cherry — Dazaifu
Tenmangū, 40 minutes from Fukuoka, is one of Japan's great plum sites.

**Budget.** Not set. Price filters stay optional everywhere in the tooling
until it is.
