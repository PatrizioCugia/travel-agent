# Nagoya November 2026

Solo, eleven nights, twelve days. Open-jaw CPH → NGO, KIX → CPH. Mid-to-late
November, dates locked once flights are booked.

**Structure**: seven nights in Nagoya (Anshin Oyado Sakae, capsule + onsen,
free curry/ramen in the lobby), two nights in Tsumago (Maruya minshuku, since
1789, dinner+breakfast included), two nights in Okuhida (Yamano-iori onsen
minshuku, Shin-Hirayu Onsen).

**Thesis**: Nagoya base for dense food-and-craft urban exploration, contrasted
against four nights of rural minshuku in preserved Edo post town (Tsumago)
and mountain onsen (Okuhida). Peak November Korankei autumn maples + night
illumination is the trip's singular seasonal experience — what can ONLY
happen this season this year.

**Singular-experience-per-trip filter**:

- Korankei autumn maples + night illumination (Day 7) — mid-late November only
- Maruya Tsumago minshuku (Nights 8–9) — 1789 Edo post-town inn
- Yamano-iori Okuhida onsen-minshuku (Nights 10–11) — Northern Japanese Alps
- First arrival in Japan (Day 1) — trip-specific
- Nayabashi Yoichi night market on 4th Friday (likely Nov 27) — date-specific

**Day trips from Nagoya**: Tokoname (pottery), Seki (knives), Korankei
(autumn maples), Hikone (castle + Omi beef on the transit day to Tsumago).
