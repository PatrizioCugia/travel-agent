Last bath before breakfast at [[yamano-iori-hotaka-so]]. The okami sees me off with a small package of pickles for the road. Bus down out of the mountains, the same switchbacks in reverse — already the air thickening as the altitude drops.

At [[takayama-station]] I board the Wide View Hida south, the third premium ekiben of the trip in my lap: Hida-gyu thermal bento. You pull the cord, the box warms itself, and the beef comes out tender over rice, the fat just rendered. The Kiso gorges roll past again, this time in the opposite direction, and the trip rewinds itself through the window.

Change at Nagoya for the Haruka to [[kansai-airport-kix]]. Twelve days compressed into the time it takes to cross Osaka Bay: hitsumabushi at Houraiken, the smell of cedar in Atsuta, the red maples at Korankei under floodlight, the creak of [[maruya-tsumago]]'s floors, the Alps from 2,156 metres this morning. Nothing left undone in the way that matters.

Check-in, security, a last vending-machine matcha. The flight home.
