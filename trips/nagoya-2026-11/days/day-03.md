## Morning

Meitetsu train south to Tokoname — about an hour, a soft day after Day 2. Tokoname is a pottery town that still smells like a pottery town: kilns, clay, woodsmoke off the older studios. Walk the Pottery Footpath slowly.

## Lunch + afternoon

Lunch wherever the walk lands — [[tokoname-local-lunch]] picks itself, a small place along the path with grilled fish or a set menu. The afternoon belongs to [[tokoname-pottery-studios]]: kyusu and yunomi for home, a small tea cup for Grandma, a sake cup for Dad. The point isn't to buy a lot; it's to handle a lot until two pieces feel inevitable. INAX showrooms nearby if there's time and the legs hold.

## Dinner

Back to Nagoya in the early evening. [[yamamotoya-honten-esca]] in the ESCA arcade under the station — miso-nikomi udon in a black, almost bitter Hatcho miso broth, simmered in its own earthenware pot until the lid is the bowl. After a cool day in a pottery village, eating from a clay pot is on theme without trying. Early night.
