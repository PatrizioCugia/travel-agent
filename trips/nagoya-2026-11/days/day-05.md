## Morning

JR + Nagaragawa Railway to Seki — about 90 minutes. Seki has been forging blades for 800 years and it shows in the seriousness of the small shops around the [[seki-hamono-museum-shop]]. The mission is two knives: a gyuto and a petty, both takeable as carry-on, both worth the testing-on-paper ritual at the counter.

## Lunch

[[seki-local-lunch]] near the museum — soba or unagi, whichever the day produces.

## Afternoon

Back to Nagoya by late afternoon, drop the knives at [[anshin-oyado-sakae]], onsen, reset.

## Dinner

[[sakana-no-hachibei]] in Yabacho — a premium seafood izakaya, the kind of place where the day's fish list is the menu. Booked a week or two out. Slow it down here.

## Night

If the evening still has legs, one of: [[bar-hasegawa]] for a classic cocktail, [[bar-lumina]] as the alternative, or — if it's Tuesday through Saturday — [[pettegola]] for an authentic Sapporo-style shime parfait, which Nagoya does not otherwise do.
