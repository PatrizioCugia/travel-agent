## Morning

Early to [[yanagibashi-market]] — Nagoya's seafood wholesale market. Breakfast is sashimi and rice while the fishmongers are still tearing through boxes. From there, walk north along [[horikawa-river-walk]] to [[shikemichi-district]], the preserved merchant quarter with black-walled storehouses tucked behind the station. Quiet, low, very little tourist traffic.

## Lunch

[[kanazawa-maimon-sushi]] in Sakae for kaiten lunch — premium Sea of Japan fish at conveyor prices. Save the appetite a little.

## Afternoon

[[kawabun]] in Marunouchi for the seven-matcha tasting parfait — 400 years of restaurant pedigree, weekdays only 13:00–17:30 (this is why Day 4 has to land on a weekday). Drift afterward through [[sankodo-pens]] for the Tombow Zoom 707 and Sakura Craft Lab 002 — pen stop for brother and self. Optional sake at [[sake-stand-sabou]] standing-style, or a flat white at [[trunk-coffee]] near Horikawa to reset before dinner.

## Dinner

[[sushi-okimuraya]] in Higashi-sakura — Edomae omakase, eight seats, booked weeks ahead. This is the formal sushi night of the trip. Don't drink much before. Walk back along the river.
