## Morning

[[konparu-kissaten]] for the full Nagoya kissaten breakfast — egg sandwich, ogura toast, thick coffee, vinyl booth. This is the city's morning vernacular and worth eating into slowly.

## Late morning

Walk to [[osu-kannon]] and into the Osu arcade. If the date is the 18th or 28th, the [[osu-antique-market]] is on the temple grounds — first stop, before crowds. Then a long, unstructured loop: [[mandarake-osu]] for vintage manga and figures, [[animate-osu]] for the mainstream side, two action figures into the bag. [[komehyo-osu]]'s Floor 4 for a vintage haori; [[unwave-osu]], [[archaic-osu]], and [[crout-osu]] in whatever order energy dictates — workwear and denim are the through-line.

## Lunch

[[mitsumura-tempura]] in Imaike — tempura kakiage with a 50-year-old sauce. Quick, sharp, light.

## Afternoon

[[masuhan-chaten]] in Fushimi for matcha ice cream — 1840 tea shop, reopened 2023, exactly the kind of new-old Patrizio likes. Or [[komeda-coffee]] for the red velvet sofa and the kissaten chain that Nagoya invented.

## Dinner

[[setsugekka-tanaka-satoru]] for yakiniku. Tabelog Award Bronze, premium cuts, slow grill, no hurry — book three to four weeks ahead.

## Night

[[zenkoku-meishu-izakaya]] for one or two glasses off the 100+ Aichi sake list, then [[25-ji-made-ice]] for a takeout parfait on the walk home. Bed late.
