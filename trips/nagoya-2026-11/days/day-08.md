Check-out morning at [[anshin-oyado-sakae]], pack heavy — the next two nights are minshuku territory. Grab the premium ekiben from [[takashimaya-depachika]] at [[nagoya-station]] for the Shinano ride later, then catch the train to Hikone.

## Morning

[[hikone-castle]] is the point of the detour: one of only twelve original keeps left in Japan, a National Treasure that survived the Meiji demolitions and the war. Climb the steep wooden stairs to the top floor — the views over Lake Biwa are the payoff. Drop down into [[genkyu-en]] afterward, the daimyo's garden tucked beneath the castle walls, quiet on a weekday morning.

## Lunch

Walk down Yume Kyobashi Castle Road for [[hikone-omi-beef]]. Omi is one of the three historic wagyu lineages — older than Kobe, harder to find outside Shiga. A short course at lunch, not a dinner blowout. Just enough.

## Afternoon → Evening

Back to the station, board the Shinano east. Open the depachika bento somewhere around Gifu — kaiseki by train window, fifty minutes of fields and river. Off at [[nakatsugawa-station]], then the local bus into the Kiso valley. By dusk, [[maruya-tsumago]] — a minshuku running since 1789, dark wood, paper screens, irori smoke. Dinner is set, served in your room. The city is already very far away.
