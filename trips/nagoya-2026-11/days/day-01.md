Land at [[nagoya-airport-ngo]] mid-afternoon. The Mu-Sky train into the city is the first soft landing — drop the bag at [[anshin-oyado-sakae]], take the onsen, accept that the body is on Copenhagen time and the city is already past lunch.

## Dinner

Walk to [[wakashachiya]] for curry udon. This is the right first meal: not a temple of a dish, just thick noodles in a fragrant, faintly sweet curry broth that signals "you are in Nagoya now." Light enough to eat after a 12-hour day, hot enough to reset the body. Sit at the counter, order without ceremony, leave full.

## Night

Back to the capsule. The lobby has free curry and ramen on tap until late — useful intel for the rest of the week, but tonight just stack a small bowl, shower, sleep early. The point of Day 1 is to not break Day 2.
